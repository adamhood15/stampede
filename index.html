How to Play

Stampede

Complete the word

Eight letters are scattered down the flume, offered one at a time. Bank all eight in a single run to finish the ride and unlock the reveal. Miss one and it comes back around — but the run has to last.

Grab Buckaroos

Coins are pure score. They cost you nothing to take and nothing to miss, so chase them for the leaderboard.

Dodge, jump, duck

Every hazard has one right answer. Hit one and it costs a tube — three and the run is over.

Controls

← →
Slide Hold an arrow key and he keeps sliding that way — tap it for a nudge. Press and HOLD anywhere, then move your thumb left and right. He follows it like a dial, so you can sit between lanes instead of snapping between them.
↑
Jump Up arrow, W or Space. Flick up, or tap — you can do it mid-slide without letting go.
↓
Duck Down arrow or S. Hold it to stay flat. Flick down. Holding your thumb low keeps you tucked.

The Buttons

The controls tucked around the edge of the screen.

MuteKills the music and effects — tap again to bring them back.
BackLeaves whatever screen you're on without touching the run.
PlayDrops you back into a paused run.
PauseHolds the run right where it is.
